Edexcel IGCSE Physics 4PH1 — Year 10 content review

Time allowed: 70 minutes | Total marks: 64

Instructions

  • Answer all questions.
  • You may use a calculator and a ruler.
  • Show all steps in your calculations and state the units.
  • Where a question asks you to state the formula, write the formula down.
  • If you get stuck, move on and come back. Don't leave something blank that you half-know.

This is not an exam. Nobody is grading it. Its only job is to find out which topics need work, so the next three weeks get spent on the right things. Write what you actually think.

Section A — Forces and Motion

A1 State the equation linking weight, mass and gravitational field strength. (1)

A2 A car increases its speed from 8.0 m/s to 20 m/s in 4.0 seconds. Calculate its acceleration. (2)

acceleration = ......................... m/s²

A3 A force of 40 N acts on a spanner at a perpendicular distance of 25 cm from the pivot. Calculate the moment of the force. Give your answer in newton metres (N m). (2)

moment = ......................... N m

A4 A skydiver jumps from a plane and eventually falls at a constant speed, known as terminal velocity. Explain, in terms of forces, why the skydiver reaches terminal velocity. (3)

Section B — Energy

B1 State the principle of conservation of energy. (1)

B2 A motor is supplied with 800 J of electrical energy. It transfers 240 J usefully to the kinetic store of a load. Calculate the efficiency of the motor. (2)

efficiency = ......................... %

B3 A crane lifts a 3.0 kg mass through a height of 2.5 m. Calculate the gain in gravitational potential energy. (Take g = 10 N/kg.) (2)

GPE = ......................... J

B4 A ball is dropped and falls to the ground. Describe the energy transfers that take place, and explain why the kinetic energy of the ball just before it lands is less than the gravitational potential energy it had at the start. (3)

below: ......................... above: .........................

C4 (a) Describe the difference between a transverse wave and a longitudinal wave. You may draw a diagram to help your answer. (2)

C4 (b) State one harmful effect on the human body of excessive exposure to ultraviolet radiation. (1)

Section D — Static Electricity

D1 (b) State the charge left on the cloth. (1)

D2 Name the piece of laboratory apparatus used to detect whether an object is electrostatically charged. (1)

D3 When an aircraft is refuelled there is a danger of an explosion caused by static electricity. Explain how this danger arises and how it is reduced. (3)
