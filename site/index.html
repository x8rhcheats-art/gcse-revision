Start hereHow to use this

Each module has four parts, and they're meant to be done in order.

  1. What comes up — which questions on this topic appeared in the 2023, 2024 and 2025 papers, and how many marks they were worth. Read this first so you know what you're aiming at.
  2. Cheat sheet — equations, definitions in the exact wording the mark schemes accept, and the specific phrases that lose marks. This is the part to reread the night before.
  3. Understanding it — slow questions designed to break the common misconceptions. Not exam questions. Think properly before opening the answers; reading them straight away wastes them.
  4. Exam drill — exam-register questions with full mark-by-mark answers. One minute per mark.

The red boxes throughout are quotes from the examiners who marked these papers at the school. They are unusually specific about where marks went, and they are the reason this pack exists in the shape it does. Read them. They are worth more than any general revision advice.
